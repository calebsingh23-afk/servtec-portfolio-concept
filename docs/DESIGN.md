# Design notes

The working motorcycle and its delivery box anchor the page. The service choice is the useful interaction: visitors can move directly from a service guide to the relevant demo enquiry.

| Scene | Visual story | Website copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Introduction | Readable headline, visible actions and isolated motorcycle | Keep your fleet moving. |
| Movement | Small perspective shift with sequential labels | Fleet rental / Servicing & repairs / Parts & fabrication |
| Resolution | Bike settles and the short sticky interval ends | The right support. For the road ahead. |

The existing buildless site and core copy were preserved. Services are easier to scan in wide rows, the company introduction uses an explicitly conceptual blue typographic panel, and the enquiry journey uses open columns instead of another repeated split section.

Self-review after screenshots: the product silhouette and headline are clear focal points; desktop and mobile margins align; the form stays honest. Limitations are the absence of verified workshop photography and the deliberately subtle still-image motion. No gallery-level score or independent design endorsement is claimed.
